## What changed

A chess result and a card result can be entered. The release before this one said what a tournament is and deliberately removed the wrong way of recording a result without building the right one, so since 0.68.0 a match in one of those tournaments could be scheduled but never scored. This builds the model that scores it, from the parts a match is made of up to the table that ranks on them. The practice round of the cultural quiz also stops being three questions written into the code.

A match in a series tournament is a series of parts. The tournament says how many parts a match holds, whether it plays them all or stops when one side has enough, how a part is decided, and what one part is called in the game's own words. A chess match is two games decided by a win, a draw or a loss. A maryass match is three rounds played to a hundred points where the first side to two takes the match. Combinations that make no sense are refused where they are set rather than left to fail later, so a points target on a part decided by outcome, a target on a free score, and a number of parts to win that a match can never reach are all turned away, and nothing can be changed once a match has been played.

The result of a match is worked out rather than stored. One function walks a match's parts in order and says where it stands, how many parts are left, whether it is over and who won it. Nothing holds a total, because a total would have to hold half a point and the two columns that carry a football score are whole numbers. Totals are kept in half points inside that walk and are signed, so a side can stand below nothing and win its way back.

Home and away stay for football only. A chess match played at one table has no away side, and the pair was being used to mean the two sides of a match, which is not what its name says. A series match now records its two sides in columns that claim no venue, and one place asks the tournament's shape and hands the rest of the app the right pair. A football admin still reads المضيف and الضيف. A series admin reads the first side and the second side, and the screen says that order is the draw's and gives neither side an advantage.

Chess and dhamet get their colours. The tournament says whether its two sides are told apart and what each of them is called, the draw hands out the opening colour and spreads it across the round so one player does not open every match, and the colours turn over on every part after the first. A match of an even number of parts comes out with each side opening half the time, and that is checked rather than assumed. A match of a single part says the balance cannot cover it instead of appearing balanced. A level knockout match is extended by another pair of parts, and again while it stays level, which resolves the tie and keeps the colours even by the same rule rather than by two that could drift apart.

The maryass teysse is built as a move the tournament declares rather than as a flag in the code. A tournament says what its moves are called in its own words and what each one does, how many parts to the side that did it and how many from the other. A match records instances of them, and the same walk that reads the parts reads these alongside them in the order they happened. The move lands immediately and ends the part being played, which is why a part can exist, be marked abandoned and score nothing. An admin can undo one while the match is unfinished, and undoing it takes the part it ended. تيس is not translated anywhere.

**A match can be won by a teysse.** In maryass the first side to two parts takes the match and the move is worth two, so a side that does one from nothing stands at two and the match is over without the remaining parts being played, with the other side at minus two. That follows from the four rules the move already had rather than being a new decision, and it is what the code does.

The result form asks for a part at a time. It shows the parts already recorded, says where the match stands and what would end it, refuses a part that does not fit the tournament's mode and says which number or outcome it wanted, and lets a part be corrected or removed while the match is unfinished. The match card shows the parts rather than only the total, a match still being played reads differently from a finished one, and a level knockout match being extended says so instead of looking finished and wrong. The public card and the shared image show what the admin sees. Half points render as ½ throughout, never as a decimal and never rounded, and a negative total reads left to right so the minus sign stays in front of the number rather than trailing it in an Arabic line.

The table ranks a series tournament on its own terms. Parts for, parts against and part difference in place of the goal columns, and points that are the parts a side took rather than three for a win, carrying halves. Where the parts are played to a points threshold only the parts count in the table, because a hundred to sixty and a hundred to ninety nine are the same part won and a points column would be on a different scale in every tournament. What separates two sides level on points is head to head, then part difference, then parts for, then the fewest cards, which is the football order with the goal terms replaced so an admin reading two tables in one app holds one rule. A football table is untouched.

The practice round of the quiz is edited rather than deployed. Its three questions move into a question bank with a well known id, seeded by the migration so nothing goes blank, and the existing bank editor edits them. Questions can be put in the order a member meets them, which matters because the third one is worth double. The clock the practice round runs on sits with the other quiz settings and is validated by the same rule the competitions use. When the bank is empty the practice round is simply not offered, so nobody opens a round with no questions in it, and the picker says how many questions it holds instead of always saying three.

## What is not there yet

**A series tournament still has to be configured over the API rather than from a screen.** Everything a chess or a card tournament needs is stored, validated and refused correctly, and every screen reads it, but the setup form does not yet ask for the number of parts, how the match ends, how a part is decided, the word for one part, the colours, or the moves the tournament declares. Until that form exists an admin can enter a result only for a tournament whose configuration was set another way, and the result form points at a details tab that does not carry those controls yet. That is the first thing to build on top of this.

## Why

0.68.0 named the shape of a tournament and took the football apparatus off the ones that were never football, and it stopped there on purpose, because a half model of a chess result would have cost more to undo than it saved. The gap it left is the reason this release exists, and closing it is the whole of it.

Two things settled before the work started reach almost every part of it, and both are cheap now and expensive later. A total can go negative, because a side that takes a swing against it stands below nothing and has to win that back, so nothing floors at zero. And parts and moves interleave in time, so where a match stands is a walk in order and never two sums added together. Both were built in from the first change that had a total in it.

The practice round is unrelated to any of that. It was in the release because it is small, it proves the same loop, and changing a word of it should not need a deploy.

## How it was tested

Every pull request ran the four tiers on its own before it merged.

The walk that decides a match was written first, as a function with no database behind it, and everything else reads it. It is covered at its edges. A part won, drawn and lost. An abandoned part scoring nothing while still counting as one of the parts the match holds. A match that stops the moment one side reaches the number that ends it, and a part recorded after that not counting. A side driven below nothing. A match won by a move on its own. Two moves by one side, one from each side, and a move early in a match that would otherwise have been won later, which is the case that shows the walk is chronological rather than two sums.

The schema move was driven over the wire rather than only typechecked. A football match created by hand lands on home and away with the series pair empty, a series match lands on the series pair with home and away empty, editing the sides of a series match moves its own pair, the draw writes the right pair for each shape, and deleting an entrant takes its match with it. A series knockout was drawn for four entrants, given its parts, and advanced to a final on the parts rather than on a score.

Every refusal was driven over the wire too. A setup that makes no sense, a part that does not fit the mode, a part played to a target that never reached it, a match that will take no more parts, a move nobody declared, and a move recorded after the match is over.

Screens were read at 360px rather than assumed. The result form with five parts, colours on, one part abandoned and one side on a negative total. The match card in two shapes, a chess match of five parts and a card match of three where one side is below nothing. The moves panel with a move recorded and the part it ended. The two pickers on the new match form in both vocabularies.

The minus sign was looked at rather than trusted. Rendered the ordinary way inside an Arabic line, a negative half comes out with the sign trailing the number and reading as something else. The two renderings were put side by side, and every total now sits in an element that reads left to right.
